## What

xOne cannot trace a call that is not in a block. `debug_traceCall` answers `-32601`. Wallet simulators, security tools and dapp debuggers call it before they send a transaction.

## Why

8 of the 10 selected competitors have this capability (file evidence at the audited commit):

- `ethereum/go-ethereum`: [eth/tracers/api.go](https://github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum/blob/8059ff8beb3170eb8b977169ac51bb0423c97ee1/eth/tracers/api.go)
- `bnb-chain/bsc`: [eth/tracers/api.go](https://github.com/bnb-chain/bsc/blob/54afaff4da31ac9e49ba646a4f65104edac5a2f3/eth/tracers/api.go)
- `0xPolygon/bor`: [eth/tracers/api.go](https://github.com/0xPolygon/bor/blob/ee09efd5dd9ef75917368990b272581b0e2c2665/eth/tracers/api.go)
- `XinFinOrg/XDPoSChain`: [eth/tracers/api.go](https://github.com/XinFinOrg/XDPoSChain/blob/cb1ef79073c05f50b9df75b1e740387b9d72af99/eth/tracers/api.go)
- `erigontech/erigon`: [rpc/jsonrpc/tracing.go](https://github.com/erigontech/erigon/blob/bbb5524e53fb057e7d47a5066e152f1091ac4ba4/rpc/jsonrpc/tracing.go)
- `paradigmxyz/reth`: [crates/rpc/rpc-api/src/debug.rs](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/blob/36600d5ff9628465475dd4c7608e2e9f2fe24273/crates/rpc/rpc-api/src/debug.rs)

- `besu-eth/besu`: [ethereum/api/src/main/java/org/hyperledger/besu/ethereum/api/jsonrpc/internal/methods/DebugTraceCall.java](https://github.com/besu-eth/besu/blob/2b839d96863d01210f45a6f62127e6229e5b0889/ethereum/api/src/main/java/org/hyperledger/besu/ethereum/api/jsonrpc/internal/methods/DebugTraceCall.java)

Priority: P1: 8 of 10 selected competitors have it (rule: 8+ = P1).

Our code today: none (crates/xone-rpc/src/debug.rs serves traceTransaction/traceBlock only; spec/rpc/scope-adr-008.md: out).

Related xOneRS issues: #42. This issue adds the capability; it does not replace their gates.

## 80% of the way to

2. Accept `stateOverrides` and `blockOverrides` in `traceConfig` once GAP overrides lands (shared parser).
3. Class C3, the same deadline and cancel flag as the other traces.
4. Tests: compare `callTracer` and `prestateTracer` output with the xdc02 archive for 100 calls.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 100 traced calls equal the xdc02 archive output.
- [ ] Cancel on timeout is tested.

## ASD-STE100

1. The node MUST serve `debug_traceCall` with `callTracer` and `prestateTracer`.
2. The output MUST be equal to geth-XDC output for the same call and block.
3. The trace MUST stop when the deadline expires or the client disconnects.
