## What

`eth_call` and `eth_estimateGas` ignore the third parameter. Integrators cannot override balance, nonce, code or storage of an account, or the block number, time and base fee. Multicall-style readers, gas estimators for undeployed contracts and simulators depend on it.

## Why

9 of the 10 selected competitors have this capability (file evidence at the audited commit):

- `ethereum/go-ethereum`: [internal/ethapi/override/override.go](https://github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum/blob/8059ff8beb3170eb8b977169ac51bb0423c97ee1/internal/ethapi/override/override.go)
- `XinFinOrg/XDPoSChain`: [internal/ethapi/api.go](https://github.com/XinFinOrg/XDPoSChain/blob/cb1ef79073c05f50b9df75b1e740387b9d72af99/internal/ethapi/api.go)
- `bnb-chain/bsc`: [internal/ethapi/api.go](https://github.com/bnb-chain/bsc/blob/54afaff4da31ac9e49ba646a4f65104edac5a2f3/internal/ethapi/api.go)
- `0xPolygon/bor`: [internal/ethapi/api.go](https://github.com/0xPolygon/bor/blob/ee09efd5dd9ef75917368990b272581b0e2c2665/internal/ethapi/api.go)
- `paradigmxyz/reth`: [crates/rpc/rpc-eth-api/src/core.rs](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/blob/36600d5ff9628465475dd4c7608e2e9f2fe24273/crates/rpc/rpc-eth-api/src/core.rs)

Priority: P1: 9 of 10 selected competitors have it (rule: 8+ = P1).

Our code today: crates/xone-rpc/src/eth.rs (call/estimate read params 0 and 1 only).

Related xOneRS issues: #42. This issue adds the capability; it does not replace their gates.

## 80% of the way to

1. `crates/xone-rpc/src/eth.rs`: parse `stateOverride` (balance, nonce, code, state, stateDiff, movePrecompileToAddress) and `blockOverrides` (number, time, gasLimit, coinbase, baseFee) with geth's field names and errors.
2. `crates/xone-executor/src/overlay.rs`: apply the overrides to a throw-away overlay above the block state; wide balances accepted.
3. Same parser serves `debug_traceCall` and `eth_simulateV1`.
4. Tests: vectors from geth `internal/ethapi/override` cases; byte-compare 100 calls with the xdc02 archive.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] 100 override calls equal the xdc02 archive.
- [ ] Overrides never reach the persisted state (test).

## ASD-STE100

1. The node MUST apply state overrides only to the call. It MUST NOT change the stored state.
2. The node MUST refuse `state` and `stateDiff` together for one account, as geth-XDC does.
3. Answers with overrides MUST be equal to geth-XDC answers for the same input.
