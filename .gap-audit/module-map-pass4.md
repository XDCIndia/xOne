Authentication is transport/method policy and signer ownership, not customer roles. Jobs are Tokio tasks and node timers, not billing cron. Integrations include devp2p, snap/1, xpre/1, ethstats, SkyNet and the conformance oracle. Financial UI categories in the original generic brief are not applicable to this product.
