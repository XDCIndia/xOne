# Special transactions: a sign transaction is admitted only from a candidate, penalised ones included, skips the price floor and goes straight to pending

Lines are `XDCIndia/go-ethereum` at `6fc8e8c2`. Rust: `xone_builder::txpool::special` and `validate`.

**Rule.**

1. A special transaction is a transaction whose target is 0x89 (block signers) or 0x90 (randomize), at any block (`types.IsSpecialTx`, `core/types/transaction.go:740-742`; `xone_evm::route::is_special_tx`). This page says "sign transaction" for 0x89.
2. **Who may send one.** Only an address that `IsAuthorisedAddress` accepts (`eth/backend.go:475-484`, wired at `:480`; `consensus/XDPoS/xdpos_api.go:195-212`). For a V2 head that is **every address of the epoch's candidate pool** (`snap.CandidatePool()`, the gap snapshot's `NextEpochCandidates`): masternodes, standby candidates and **penalised** candidates. A penalised node can therefore still send and relay sign transactions, which is how it comes back (`spec/consensus/penalties-and-comeback.md`). For a V1 head it is the masternodes of the checkpoint. While the downloader is running nobody is authorised (`eth/backend.go:479`). A special transaction from anyone else is refused in `ValidateTxBasics`: `special transaction sender is not a signer` (`validation.go:152-156`).
   - Rust: `Authority::Candidates(set)` or `Authority::Syncing`. The caller builds the set from `xone_consensus::v2::gap_snapshot::SnapshotV2::candidates` (V2) or the checkpoint masternodes (V1) and calls `Pool::set_authority` when the epoch changes. The pool has no unset mode: a pool that was given no authority refuses every special transaction.
3. **Price exemption.** A special transaction is exempt from the pool minimum tip (`validation.go:198-213`), from the tier floor (`:406-421`, `legacypool.go:585-603`), and from the packer's tip filter (`Pending`, `legacypool.go:539-551`: a list is cut at the first regular transaction below the minimum tip, never at a special one). Its gas price is 0 by construction (`contracts.CreateTxSign`).
4. **Fast path** (`legacypool.go:960-1015`). A special transaction that is from an authorised sender **and** has a known calldata shape goes straight to pending, past the fill checks and the price checks, when its nonce is the sender's pending nonce:
   - 0x89: exactly 68 bytes that start with `e341eaa4` (`sign(uint256,bytes32)`);
   - 0x90: at least 4 bytes that start with `34d38600` (`setSecret`) or `e11f5ba2` (`setOpening`) (`hasKnownSpecialCalldata`, `:924-939`; the selectors are `common/types.go:66-68`).
   Any other calldata, or a nonce that is not the pending nonce, takes the ordinary path: it is still exempt from the minimum tip and the floor (steps above) but is subject to the slot limits, the price bump and the queue.
5. **`promoteSpecialTx`** (`legacypool.go:793-837`). The transaction is put in the sender's executable list at its nonce. If a special transaction is already there and is not an expired sign transaction it is `duplicate a special transaction`. A sign transaction is expired when the head is past `block + 2 * epoch`, `block` read from calldata bytes 4 to 36, `BlockSigner.sol:21` (`isSigningTxExpired`, `:860-891`: not expired when the calldata is under 68 bytes or the epoch is 0). An expired one is replaced, also when the new transaction's nonce is **below** the pending nonce (`:985-1013`). The pending nonce only goes up (`setIfHigher`).
6. **Replacement against a regular transaction** (`list.go:303-340`): a special transaction takes the nonce of a regular one at any price; a regular transaction never replaces a special one. Two special transactions follow the ordinary price bump (so a second one at the same nonce is `replacement transaction underpriced` unless the first expired).
7. A special transaction that went to pending is announced to peers like any new executable transaction.

**Deviation from geth: the list total.** `promoteSpecialTx` puts the transaction in the list without adding its cost to `list.totalcost` (`:827-834`). The cost of a sign transaction with value 0 is 0, so nothing shows. With value 1 or more (the balance check lets it in) the removal of the transaction when a block includes it subtracts a cost that was never added, and `subTotalCost` panics (`list.go:496-505`, `totalcost underflow`). The panic is in the reorg goroutine and ends the node. The oracle shows it (`pin_crash` in `scenarios.json`: `crashed, panic: totalcost underflow`), and any candidate can send such a transaction. xOne adds the cost (`List::put_special`) and saturates the subtraction; test `pinned_pool_crash_on_included_sign_tx_with_value_does_not_crash_here`. This is the only place the Rust pool behaves differently from the pin on the oracle scripts. Worth reporting upstream.

**Vectors.**

- `conformance/vectors/txpool/special-apothem.json` and `special-net2040.json` (built by `build/special.py` from `xdc02`, read-only): head 85,245,600 (Apothem) and 743,700 (net2040), each in the middle of an epoch. Inputs: the 0x88 storage words of the epoch's gap block (85,244,850 and 742,950), the three lists of `XDPoS_getMasternodesByNumber` (Apothem 99 masternodes, 35 penalised, 0 standby; net2040 13, 1, 0) and, for Apothem, 8 real sign transactions of the window (4 from masternodes, 4 from penalised candidates), rebuilt from the RPC fields (the hash and the recovered sender are checked). Tests `crates/xone-builder/tests/archive_candidates.rs`: the pool computed from the words (`gap_snapshot::build`) equals the union of the three lists on both networks; every real sign transaction is admitted and promoted at the head just before its block, and refused once its sender is taken out of the pool; all 35 penalised candidates are admitted and promoted, and addresses outside the pool are refused.
- net2040 is past its Amsterdam block, for which xone-evm has no gas rules, so the pool cannot be opened there: the net2040 test checks the pool equality, the recovered senders and the authority decision only. No real sign transaction of a penalised net2040 candidate was in the epoch's 300 blocks.
- Oracle scenarios `special-*` (signer fast path, non-signer, syncing, nonce gap, duplicate, expired and its boundary, bad calldata, 0x90, regular versus special, full pool) and the seeded ones, which mix special transactions in: equal to the pinned pool after every step.
- `crates/xone-builder/tests/rules.rs`: `a_sign_tx_from_a_candidate_is_promoted_and_one_from_anyone_else_is_refused`, `a_special_tx_skips_the_base_fee_floor_and_a_regular_one_below_it_is_refused`, `a_candidate_pool_with_penalised_members_authorises_all_of_them`.

**Not covered.** The mixed-client testnet check that canonical masternodes include a xOne-produced sign transaction (XB-23 and the mint gate). The V1 authority set is a parameter; no V1 vector was built.
