## What

An operator cannot limit discovery and dialing to private networks. geth-XDC `--netrestrict` takes a CIDR list; private XDC networks, subnets and validator sentry set-ups use it to keep validators off the public internet.

## Why

6 of the 10 selected competitors have this capability (file evidence at the audited commit):

- `ethereum/go-ethereum`: [cmd/utils/flags.go](https://github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum/blob/8059ff8beb3170eb8b977169ac51bb0423c97ee1/cmd/utils/flags.go)
- `bnb-chain/bsc`: [cmd/utils/flags.go](https://github.com/bnb-chain/bsc/blob/54afaff4da31ac9e49ba646a4f65104edac5a2f3/cmd/utils/flags.go)
- `0xPolygon/bor`: [cmd/utils/flags.go](https://github.com/0xPolygon/bor/blob/ee09efd5dd9ef75917368990b272581b0e2c2665/cmd/utils/flags.go)
- `XinFinOrg/XDPoSChain`: [cmd/utils/flags.go](https://github.com/XinFinOrg/XDPoSChain/blob/cb1ef79073c05f50b9df75b1e740387b9d72af99/cmd/utils/flags.go)
- `erigontech/erigon`: [cmd/utils/flags.go](https://github.com/erigontech/erigon/blob/bbb5524e53fb057e7d47a5066e152f1091ac4ba4/cmd/utils/flags.go)
- `paradigmxyz/reth`: [crates/net/network-types/src/peers/config.rs](https://github.com/paradigmxyz/reth/blob/36600d5ff9628465475dd4c7608e2e9f2fe24273/crates/net/network-types/src/peers/config.rs)

Priority: P2: 6 of 10 selected competitors have it (rule: 5-7 = P2).

Our code today: none.

Related xOneRS issues: #32. This issue adds the capability; it does not replace their gates.

## 80% of the way to

1. `bin/xone/src/flags.rs`, `crates/xone-node/src/config.rs`: `--netrestrict <cidr,...>`.
2. `crates/xone-net/src/discv4.rs`: drop neighbours and pings from outside the list; `crates/xone-net/src/peers/mod.rs`: refuse inbound and outbound connections outside the list (static and trusted peers included, as geth).
3. Tests: two devnet nodes in different ranges do not connect; a node inside the range connects.

Acceptance criteria:

- [ ] Devnet test with in-range and out-of-range peers.

## ASD-STE100

1. When `--netrestrict` is set, the node MUST NOT connect to a peer outside the listed ranges.
2. Discovery MUST NOT add a node outside the listed ranges to the table.
